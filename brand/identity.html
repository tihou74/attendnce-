01

Colour

الألوان

The group's colour is #A89060 — it fills 86.3% of the Al Sheikha logo. Its contrast on white is only 3.08:1, so it cannot be used for text on white (4.5:1 is required). It stays the identity colour for surfaces, rules and the logo; a darker step carries it into text.

02

Typography

الخطوط

IBM Plex Sans Arabic — one family carrying Arabic and Latin together. This matters here specifically: every training guide in your system is an Arabic line above an English line in the same section. With two separate fonts those two lines never sit on the same rhythm. With one family they do. IBM Plex Mono handles all money and time figures.

04

Rules

قواعد الاستخدام

✓ Do

  • Gold for surfaces, rules and the logo; Gold 800 when gold must be text.
  • Navy for body text and the primary button.
  • One primary action per screen.
  • Numbers in mono with tabular figures.
  • Each brand's own colour for branch/brand tags only.
  • Arabic line-height 1.85, Latin 1.55.

✕ Don't

  • Gold text on white — 3.08:1, fails.
  • White text on gold — 3.08:1, fails.
  • Emoji as icons; use SVG.
  • Colour alone to signal status without a word.
  • Gradients, glows, or soft large shadows.
  • A brand colour on the app chrome — the dashboard is the group's.
Style direction: Minimalism & Swiss — matched by the UI/UX Pro Max skill to enterprise dashboards (density 8/10, variance 3/10, motion 2/10). Colours measured from al-sheikha-group-logo.png, gaimer-w-kahi-logo.png, shai-bu-hamad-logo.png. Contrast per WCAG 2.1.
